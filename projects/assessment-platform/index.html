Platform leadership

Multi-tenant assessment platform at scale

Leading modernization of an assessment platform engineered for 50K+ concurrency and approximately one million tests per month.

2025 — PresentEngineering Manager · Architecture and platform leadership50K+ concurrency
Context

The product landscape

ThinkExam is a multi-tenant EdTech ecosystem spanning test administration, candidate experiences, question banks, scheduling, online and offline delivery, AI proctoring, coding and psychometric assessments, evaluation, analytics, LMS capabilities, certifications, payments, APIs, and integrations.

Challenge

What had to change

Modernize a broad, business-critical assessment ecosystem while sustaining secure delivery, high availability, and predictable performance during large concurrent examination windows.

Approach

Architecture, delivery, and leadership

  1. 01

    Set platform modernization priorities and architecture guardrails across Node.js, Laravel, Angular, React, Next.js, and GraphQL services.

  2. 02

    Used horizontal scaling, caching, queues, load balancing, container orchestration, observability, and cloud-native infrastructure to strengthen critical delivery paths.

  3. 03

    Established CI/CD, Secure SDLC, OWASP controls, VAPT remediation, engineering standards, and technical-review practices.

  4. 04

    Led approximately 20 engineers across backend, frontend, QA, DevOps, product, and platform workstreams.

Outcomes

What the work enabled

  • Platform designed to support 50K+ concurrent users.
  • More than one million assessments delivered per month.
  • Clearer architecture governance, reliability ownership, and technical-roadmap execution.
