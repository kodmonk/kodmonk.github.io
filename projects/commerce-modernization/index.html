Solution architecture

Global hosting and commerce modernization

Reshaped a high-traffic platform through API optimization, distributed caching, messaging, and a modern React delivery model.

2024 — 2025Solution Architect · Modernization and performance300K–1M monthly users
Context

The product landscape

A managed-hosting and e-commerce platform serving a large international customer base across product discovery, configuration, purchasing, account, and infrastructure journeys.

Challenge

What had to change

Improve scalability, responsiveness, and maintainability across high-traffic customer journeys without disrupting commercial operations or forcing a risky full-platform rewrite.

Approach

Architecture, delivery, and leadership

  1. 01

    Defined incremental modernization boundaries across Next.js, React, Node.js, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB.

  2. 02

    Introduced Redis caching, background workers, RabbitMQ messaging, and API optimization around high-cost workflows.

  3. 03

    Separated frontend and backend responsibilities to enable safer releases and independent performance improvements.

  4. 04

    Aligned technical sequencing with business continuity and distributed-team delivery constraints.

Outcomes

What the work enabled

  • Architecture designed for approximately 300K–1M monthly users.
  • Improved platform scalability, performance, and maintainability.
  • Modernization progressed while preserving live business operations.
