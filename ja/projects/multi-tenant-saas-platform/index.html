Context

The business landscape

Client required a modern multi-tenant EdTech SaaS platform capable of handling peak examination windows, live candidate proctoring, question bank management, and real-time evaluation.

Challenge

Technical & operational requirements

Sustain high availability, low latency, and zero data-loss during massive simultaneous user spikes while maintaining strict multi-tenant isolation.

Kodmonk Approach

  1. 01

  2. 02
