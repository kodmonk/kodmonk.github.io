La confidentialité chez Kodmonk.

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Nous expliquons la gestion des services nécessaires et optionnels. This policy explains what information this website may process, why it is processed, and the choices available to visitors. The site does not sell personal information.

Informations traitées automatiquement

Hosting and security providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, timestamps, approximate location, and diagnostic or security events to deliver and protect the site.

Analytique et gestion des balises

Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager help us understand page usage and interactions. Advertising storage and personalization are denied, and analytics load only after optional consent.

Crazy Egg may collect page views, clicks, scrolling, viewport size, and aggregated session behavior to identify usability and content problems. Contact-form content is not intentionally sent to it.

Session insights

Crazy Egg may collect page views, clicks, scrolling, viewport size, and aggregated session behavior to identify usability and content problems. Contact-form content is not intentionally sent to it.

Services de chat et de réunion

Tawk.to provides optional live chat and may process identifiers, device information, message content, and technical data needed to operate chat. Calendly processes meeting details under its own policy.

Formulaire de contact et e-mail

The contact form and public email address are disabled. Contact may be initiated through Tawk.to chat or the Calendly meeting link.

Carte de localisation

The contact page embeds Google Maps for Ghaziabad, India. Loading the map connects your browser to Google under Google’s privacy practices.

Storage, cookies, and retention

The site stores theme and privacy preferences locally. Third-party services may use cookies only after consent, with retention determined by each provider.

Vos choix

Optional integrations load only after consent. Necessary only keeps them disabled; privacy choices can be reopened from the footer.

External links and referrer privacy

Company and project links use a no-referrer policy, although destinations may collect information directly after loading.

Data requests and policy changes

You may request access to or deletion of information supplied through chat. This policy may change when site features or providers change.
